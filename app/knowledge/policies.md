# Northwind Outfitters – Support Knowledge Base

Northwind Outfitters is a fictional online store for hiking and camping gear.

## Returns
Unused items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must include original tags. Return shipping is free for store members and $6.95 for everyone else.

## Shipping
Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days and is free on orders over $50. Express shipping takes 1–2 business days and costs $15. We ship only within the United States.

## Order changes
Orders can be changed or cancelled within 1 hour of being placed. After that the order is already being packed and cannot be changed.

## Warranty
Tents, backpacks and sleeping bags have a 2-year warranty against manufacturing defects. Normal wear and damage from misuse are not covered.

## Membership
Membership is free. Members get free return shipping and early access to sales.

## Contact
Human support is available by email at support@northwind.example, Monday to Friday, 9am–5pm Eastern.
